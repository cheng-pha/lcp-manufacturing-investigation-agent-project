# Contamination Investigation Protocol

## Purpose

This procedure defines the required actions when potential contamination is identified during device inspection.

## Final Inspection

If potential contamination is identified during final inspection:

1. Stop further processing of the device.
2. Record a quality event.
3. Place the device under the appropriate quality hold.
4. Perform the required contamination investigation.
5. If contamination is confirmed, the device must be evaluated for scrap.

## Scrap Decision

A device with confirmed contamination cannot proceed to release.

The quality organization must document the event and approve the final disposition.
